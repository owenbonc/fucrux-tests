# The Automated Light

No footfall sounds upon the salted stair,
No hand has touched the brass to trim the wick,
The logbook gathers dust beyond repair,
The fog bell tolls its automated tick.

The lens revolves on motors, cold and sure,
Each sweep of light divides the dark in two,
The beam extends its calibrated cure
While cobwebs claim the window's corner view.

A helmsman reads the flash and clears the stone,
And swings his vessel seaward from the shore,
The light performs its duty all alone,
He does not ask whose eye has gone before.

The lamp tends to its work though no one stays;
The beam outlasts the hand that struck the blaze.
