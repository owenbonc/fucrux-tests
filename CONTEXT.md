# Test Project

A repository of original poems. Its language is the vocabulary used to commission a
poem precisely enough that someone else could write it and be judged right or wrong.

## Language

**Ambiguous Verdict**:
A close that withholds judgment on the loss the poem describes, sustaining both lament
and indifference as equally valid readings.
_Avoid_: open ending, unresolved, ambiguous (unqualified)

**Era-Neutral Imagery**:
Concrete images naming nothing invented after 1900, so the poem cannot be dated by its
objects.
_Avoid_: timeless, universal, abstract

**Metrical Break**:
A single line deliberately departing from the poem's meter, placed to coincide with a
turn in sense.
_Avoid_: mistake, irregularity, broken line
