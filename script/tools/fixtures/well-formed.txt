FADE IN:

INT. GLASS ROOM - NIGHT (ALPHA - DAY 1 - 09:00)

A clock with no hands. ANNA stands under it.

                         ANNA
                    This is what a clean page
                    looks like.

CUT TO:

EXT. SALT YARD - DAY (BETA - DAY 2 - 10:00)

Anna again, a world over.

                         ANNA (V.O.)
                         (quietly)
                    And this is what a second
                    one looks like.

                         BORIS
                    Then we are agreed.

INT. GLASS ROOM - DAY (ALPHA - DAY 1 - 08:00)

Earlier. The same room, the hands still on.

                         BORIS (O.S.)
                    Nothing has happened yet.

FADE OUT.

THE END
