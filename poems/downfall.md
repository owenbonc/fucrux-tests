# The Handing Over

We give the names and numbers to the book  
and find our hands are empty now, and light.  
The names we knew by heart do not come back  
and no one goes to fetch them. We sleep late.  
We follow what is drawn, and lose the north  
our feet once found: the ford, the elm, the star.  
We do not walk a country now; the earth  
is something written down. We do not steer.  
We hand the judging over too, and it goes last.  
We take the nearest answer, and the dark  
is only where we do not look. What's lost  
we cannot name; it does not stop the work.  
We hardly miss it; what we miss, we guess.  
The road is easy, and the dark is less.
